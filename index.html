Matheus Souza

Ph.D. candidate Computational Imaging KAUST

I’m a final-year Ph.D. candidate at KAUST, advised by Wolfgang Heidrich in the Computational Imaging Group. I co-design optics and neural networks: end-to-end differentiable imaging systems in which optical elements act as learned encoders, so cameras capture what a task needs rather than just pixels. My work spans minimalist camera design, deep learning for optical design, and optics-aware computational photography. In 2025, I was a Research Scientist Intern at Meta Reality Labs.

I’m on the job market for research scientist roles in computational imaging, computer vision, camera systems, and optics-aware machine learning.
